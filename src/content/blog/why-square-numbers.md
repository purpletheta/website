Square numbers have a name that comes directly from geometry. When equal rows and columns of objects are arranged to form a square, the total number of objects is a square number.

## Building a square

Arrange four counters in two rows of two. There are 2 × 2 = 4 counters. Arrange nine counters in three rows of three, and you get 3 × 3 = 9. The number of rows and the number in each row are equal.

## From area to arithmetic

A square with side length 5 units has an area of 5 × 5 = 25 square units. This is why multiplying a number by itself is called squaring, and the result is called a square number.

Square numbers connect arithmetic with shape—and help us see patterns that can be explored further.
