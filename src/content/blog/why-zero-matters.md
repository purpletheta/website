Zero may look like nothing, but it is one of the most important ideas in mathematics. It tells us that a place in a number has no value, and it also gives us a number in its own right.

## Zero as a placeholder

In the number 205, zero shows that there are no tens. Without it, 205 and 25 would be written in a way that could easily be confused. The position of each digit matters—and zero helps make that position clear.

## Zero as a number

Zero represents a quantity of none. It sits between positive and negative numbers on the number line and is the additive identity: adding zero to a number leaves that number unchanged.

## A small symbol with a big role

Zero supports place-value notation, arithmetic, algebra, coordinate systems and many ideas used in science and computing. Its story is a reminder that a simple idea can change how we understand the world.
